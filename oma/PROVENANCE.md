# Where Oma's artwork came from

Fill this in before the pictures in `character/sprites/` are published (then delete the `oma/character/sprites`
line in `.gitignore`). The whole character has the legal status of the image it comes from.

- [ ] **Who made the image, and how.** Own work, a commissioned designer, a stock asset, or an image generator.
      Name the tool or person and the date.
- [ ] **Rights.** A written assignment or licence that allows commercial use and changes. For a generated image,
      the generator's terms on commercial use and ownership, and a copy of them as they were that day.
- [ ] **Not someone else's character.** Reverse-image-search the sheet and compare it with existing robots,
      mascots and app icons. If it comes close to an existing product, change the silhouette and details until
      it does not.
- [ ] **Louis Raillé's permission.** If any code or sound from Coucou ships with Oma beyond the MIT code, his
      permission in writing (a message or an email) is kept here.
- [ ] **Name and trademark search** for the final app name, in the app stores too. The names Oma and Omi were
      only working names, and an assistant called Omi already exists.
